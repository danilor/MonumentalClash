Aztecs (promo)

Acamapichtli
When "Increasing City Size" in his city, Captives may be spent 1:1 as any resource.
May use "Tribute Empire" in cities he is in, by paying 1 Captive rather than 2 Mood tokens

Ahuitzotl
His Army ma cancel a "Hit" after a combat roll on regions with no Aztec cities (once per battle).
May sacrifice 2 Captives to "Increase City Size" in his city or build a Wonder in his city at no action cost (once per turn).

Marqzen
After an Event (not Event icon) reduces the mood or units in his city, he may reverse the mood drop an take back any or all units.
AAA: May move an adjacent enemy player Land unit 1 space to a revealed Land space not adjacent to Marqzen (once per turn and may not result in a battle).


Babylonia

Hammurabi
Civic improvement: may improve his city directly to happy by spending 1 culture token.
It costs no food or wood when using a fortress to increase city size in his city.

Nabopolassar
His army gets +2 CV against players with an equal or greater number of cities.
After any of your cities are captured, place a free infantry with Nabopolassar.

Nebuchadnezzar II
May build the Great Gardens in his city even if unrevealed (pull from deck). The cost is reduced by 1 culture token and any 2 resources.
His army gets +2 CV when attacking cities with temples.


Carthage (sea)

Hanno
Free action: may move with a group of ships if they haven't moved that turn and aren't moved again that turn (once per turn).
His ship gets +2 CV on naval battles.

Queen Dido
When with a founding unit, the unit can found a city at no activation cost.
Her city gets +2 CV if attacked. After surviving a combat round, you may eliminate her to force the attack to cease. No VP are awarded for dido this way.

Hannibal
He may reroll one combat die the first combat round, and reroll it twice if his army has fewer units. The results of any rerolls must be kept.
When elephants are with him, his entire group may ignore the mountain terrain penalty when moving.

Celts

Queen Boudica
Her army gets +2 CV vs army with more warfare advances.
When she is with a settler that founds a settlement, pay 1 food to keep the settler.

Viriatus
After each combat round he survives vs a player army: may pay 2 mood tokens to force an enemy army unit from the battle to move to an adjacent land space with no cities, units or leaders.
May play combat cards on behalf of barbarians defending or attacking from settlements within 2 spaces of Viriatus.

Vercingetorix
His army gets +1 CV for each of your settlers within 2 spaces.
When building units in his city, you may deploy each of them directly on any revealed, adjacent land spaces without enemy units, enemy cities or enemy leaders.


China

Qin Shi Huang
May build the great wall even if unrevealed. The cost is reduced by 1 culture token and any 2 resources.
Pay 2 culture tokens to cancel a hit from a combat roll (max 1 per battle).

Empress Wu Zetian
AAA activate her city to buy an Agriculture advance for 1 culture token or 1 mood token.
Get +2 CV each round in which her army has the most units.

Sun Tzu
AAA activate a city he is in to buy a warfare advance for 1 culture token or 1 mood token instead of any food.
Gets 1 culture token and 1 mood token upon winning a battle against an army in one combat round (including barbarians).


Egypt (2 barren)

Ramses II
Get 1 Culture Token or 2 ideas when building Obelisk.
His army gets +2 CV vs coastal cities and his ships get +2 CV vs Pirates.

Cleopatra
The city she is in has +1 Cultural Influence Range
Free Action: May pay and place 1 culture token in her city (once per turn). To attack any city with tokens in them, the attacking player must first pay an equal amount of culture tokens to remove them.

Imhotep
Get 1 idea after his city is activated for other purposes than collect.
It costs no food or wood when using an apothecary to increase city size in his city.


Greece

Leonidas
Get a culture token after using the draft advance in his city.
In land battles, gets +2 CV for each army unit his army is outnumbered by.

Pericles
AAA activate the city he is in to buy an education advance for 1 culture token or 1 mood token instead of any food.
His ship gets +2 CV in naval battles.

Alexander the Great
He may play "AAA" cards at no action cost by activating his city and paying 1 cultural token.
His army gets +1 CV for every region that you have a city on (except for your starting region).


Huns

Attila
Size 1 cities he is in act as one size larger when collecting or building units unless they are angry.
The first combat round, one cavalry unit in his army also gains the elephant special ability to cancel hits.

Rugila
After he captures a city, get 2 gold if there are any opponent cities (including barbarians) within 2 spaces.
Barbarian cities become happy when he attacks and captures them.

Bleda
His city acts as happy on its first activation, regardless of mood.
Opponent fortresses cannot cancel hits against his army.


India

Akbar the Great
Pay 2 ore prior to a combat roll to have each of Akbar's Elephants add +1 CV every round until they are eliminated.
Get 1 gold whenever you move Akbar into one of your cities.

Ashoka the Great
His city may reroll cultural influence attempts if it has a temple or if it tries to influence a temple (once per turn).
Gets 1 cultural token when capturing a city or 2 tokens if it has a temple.

Maharaja Sri Gupta
Advance into a new category at no action cost by activating his city and paying 1 culture token in addition to the resource cost.
His army adds 2 CV any round he does not play a combat card.


Japan

Empress Suiko
AAA (or free action if you have State Religion): Revert an influenced piece in her city (except obelisks). Does not count as a cultural influence attempt.
+2 CV if defending.

Emperor Go-Toba
Before rolling your combat dice: Pay 1 Ore to give Go-Toba's army +2 CV for the rest of the battle.
After using an academy or apothecary to increase city size in his city, you may advance at no action cost.

Emperor Jimmu
Your cities with fortresses get +2 CV every round if they are within 2 spaces of Jimmu.
After Jimmu has fought a battle, you may pay 1 culture token to reclaim an action card he spent during the battle.


Maya (2 mountain)

Wak Chanil Ajaw
Her army gets +2 CV when it is within 2 spaces of any Maya cities.
After capturing a city: May remove 1 non-settlement city-piece in your color and then increase city size in the city at no action cost and no resource cost.

Siyaj Kak
His army gets a CV bonus equal to the size of the city he attacks (first combat round only).
After his city increases city size, you may make a cultural influence attempt from the city at no action cost.

K'inich Janaab' Pakal
When moving with settlers only, he (and the group) may move 2 spaces per move action (may be used together with roads to ignore terrain).
A culture token may be spent as any 2 resources when increasing city size in his city.


Persia

Xerxes
His army has a stacking limit of 5 (may not play combat cards until he has 4 or fewer army units).
Wonders built in his city cost 2 fewer resources, culture tokens, or a mix of both.

Darius
Get 1 mood token if his city succeeds a cultural influence attempt.
May use 2 culture tokens to increase city size in his city at no action cost (once per turn).

Cyrus the Great
AAA activate his city to get 1 culture or mood token for each region with at least one of your cities (up to the size of his current city).
His combat cards may count as +2 CV instead of their normal effect. Decide after cards are revealed but before any dice are rolled.


Phoenicia (sea)

Ithobaal
After he captures a city under his cultural influence, the city becomes "happy" (after Spoils of War and Refugees).
His Army gets +2 CV when it is within 2 spaces of one of your cities with a temple.

King Hiram
When he is with a ship or a settler, it can establish a trade route that does not count towards the 4 trade routes limit.
AAA Activate a city he is in to buy a "Construction" advance for 1 culture token or 1 mood token instead of any food.

Pygmalion
One trade route traced from a settler or a ship he's with grants +1 gold or +1 culture token.
If his city is attacked, cancel a received hit by paying 1 culture token (once per battle). Reclaim token if the battle is won.


Rome

Gaius Julius Caesar
Free action: activate his city to perform a civic improvement on it
After capturing a city, may pay 1 gold to immediately "build units" at no action cost. Units built this way cannot be moved as part of the same move action as the attack.

Emperor Augustus
AAA activate his city to draw an action or objective card, then discard one of the same type (once per turn).
His army gets +2 CV on regions without your cities.

Sulla
His city cannot be the target of foreign cultural influence and any barbarian attack on cities within 2 spaces may be cancelled (even foreign cities).
Adds +2 CV vs barbarians.


Viking (sea)

Ragnar Lodbrok
Gets +2 Gold when capturing a non-angry city.
Army gets +2 CV when attacking from ship.

Eric the Red
AAA While on a ship, pay a culture token and place it on his region (max. one per region). Each counts as a 1 VP objective.
Free action: Eric and any land units on board ships in the same space as him may move to an adjacent land space.

Cnut the Great
Scoring: His city awards 0.5 VP for every space it is away from the space of your starting city.
