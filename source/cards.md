
Base Game Event Cards (18 Cards)


Card Name	Immediate Icon	Game Rules & Resolution Effects
Barbarian Incursion	Barbarians Spawn	All existing Barbarian armies on the board immediately move 1 space toward the nearest player city.
Severe Famine	Exhausted Land	Every player must pay 1 Food per city they own, or that city becomes Angry.
Epidemic	None	The player with the largest city must decrease its population size by 1 (down to a minimum of size 1).
Social Unrest	None	Every player with at least one Angry city must instantly lose 1 Culture token and 1 Mood token.
Economic Boom	Gold Mine	The active player gains 3 Gold. All other players immediately gain 1 Gold.
Cultural Breakthrough	None	The player who currently has the fewest total advances immediately gains 1 free Advance.
Pirate Raids	Barbarians Spawn	Place a Pirate ship on a sea space. All players with a Port city lose 1 resource of their choice.
Scientific Discovery	None	Every player who possesses the Academy building immediately receives 1 free Idea token.
Civil Revolt	None	The player with the most Angry cities must immediately replace one of their city buildings with a neutral Barbarian unit.
Golden Age	None	Every player whose cities are all Happy immediately receives 2 Culture tokens.
Earthquake	Exhausted Land	A random city on the board has one of its specialized buildings destroyed (turned back into a standard settlement).
Corrupt Officials	None	All players must pay 1 Gold per city they own, or those cities cannot collect resources during their next activation.
Great Philosopher	Gold Mine	The drawing player gains a unique Leader or a permanent +1 to their Idea collection actions.
Border Dispute	Barbarians Spawn	If two players share a land border, the player with lower military power must cede 1 resource to their neighbor.
Bountiful Harvest	None	Every player gets to immediately collect 2 Food from any fertile land spaces they currently occupy.
Religious Revival	None	All players who have constructed a Temple may immediately swap their current Government system for free.
Drought	Exhausted Land	All players lose access to resource harvesting on mountain and forest spaces until the end of the round.
Treasure Fleet	Gold Mine	The player with the most Ports/Ships gains 3 Gold. All other players gain 1 Gold.

Migrating Tribes	Barbarians Spawn	A new Barbarian settlement spawns, forcing any player units on that space to retreat adjacent.
Monuments of Antiquity	None	The player who has constructed the most Wonders or unique buildings immediately gains 2 Culture tokens.
Slave Revolt	None	Players utilizing specific civic/government trees must pay 1 Gold per city or face local unrest (Angry state).
Local Hero	None	The player with the lowest total army size receives a free Infantry unit in their capital city.
Tax Grievances	None	Players who collected Gold on their last turn must choose to either lose 2 Gold or turn 1 city Angry.
Dark Age	Exhausted Land	For the rest of the round, researching new Advances costs +1 Idea token across the board.
Philosophical Debate	None	Players compare total Ideas; the player with the most gains a Culture token, the player with the least loses 1 Idea.
Plague of Locusts	Exhausted Land	Agriculture and Farming advances are nullified for the turn; players cannot harvest Food from plains.
Iconoclasm	None	Religious structures are tested. Players must pay 1 Culture token or turn a city with a Temple into an Angry city.
Maritime Expansion	Gold Mine	Every player with at least one Ship on the board can immediately move that ship up to 2 spaces for free.
Mercenary Influx	None	Players can immediately spend Gold to purchase temporary Infantry units directly into an active conflict zone.
Feuding Warlords	Barbarians Spawn	Barbarian units adjacent to player borders launch immediate skirmishes against defending armies.
Typhoon	None	All Ships currently located on deep-sea spaces are destroyed unless adjacent to a coastal Port.
Currency Reform	Gold Mine	Every player may trade up to 3 standard resources (Food/Wood/Ore) for Gold at a 1:1 ratio.
Scourge of the Steppes	Barbarians Spawn	Cavalry barbarians spawn on the furthest visible ring of hexes and move toward the largest city.
Sanitation Crisis	None	Cities of size 3 or larger immediately become Angry unless the player possesses the Aqueduct or Medicine advance.
Great Fair	None	Players trade resource cards or tokens simultaneously; the player who completes the most trades gains 1 Mood token.
Intellectual Renaissance	None	The player who generates the most Ideas this turn gains a bonus permanent End-Game Victory Point card.
Vassal State Demand	None	The player with the highest total score must give 1 resource of choice to the player with the lowest score.
Provincial Rebellion	None	A settlement furthest away from a player's capital capital city instantly drops its happiness to Angry.
