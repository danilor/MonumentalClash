
Aztecs

• Captives (Tactics). Hold up to 4 defeated enemy army units here for ½ VP each at game end.
• Human Sacrifice (Rituals). Remove a Captive to cancel a newly drawn Event card, or spend Captives 1:1 as Food, Culture, or Mood tokens.
• Aztec Gold (Art & Sculptures). Once per turn, 1 Ore collected from a Mountain space may instead be taken as 1 Gold or 1 Culture token.
• Tribute Empire (Any Government). Pay 2 Mood tokens to collect from a size 3 or smaller city at no action cost once per turn.

Babylonia

• Canals (Engineering). When a city collects only 1 Food during a Collect action, gain 1 extra Food for free.
• Code of Laws (Writing). Draw an Action card after a successful Cultural Influence attempt if you have 4 or fewer Action cards.
• Star Catalogs (Astronomy). Gain 1 Idea or 1 Culture token before triggering an Event.
• Ziggurats (Dogma). Cancels the Dogma Ideas limit, and lets you spend Ideas as any resource when increasing city size with a Temple.

Carthage

• Warbeasts (Husbandry). All cities can build Elephants, and you may choose to add rolls of 1 and 2 to your Combat Value instead of canceling hits.
• Hegemony (Cartography). As a free action, found a Settlement with a Ship on an adjacent enemy-free Land space by removing the Ship and moving aboard units to the new city.
• Pirate Allies (Navigation). Your units and Leader can move through or share spaces with Pirates, treating shared Pirates as your Ships.
• Mercenaries (Draft). Spend Gold instead of Mood tokens 1:1 when using Draft, and cities with Ports may Draft Ships.

Celts

• Tribal Warfare (Tactics). Gain +1 Combat Value in Land battles against players for each Barbarian settlement within 2 spaces that holds at least 2 Barbarian units.
• Tribal Allies (Draft). Once per turn as a free action, spawn Barbarians like the Event icon, or pay 1 Food per attacking Barbarian unit to cancel a Barbarian attack.
• Druidic Influence (Priesthood). Culturally influence Barbarian settlements to place a Culture token underneath worth 1 VP until captured.
• Tribal Trade (Trade Routes). Establish trade routes with Barbarian settlements to gain Food.

China

• Rice Paddies (Irrigation). Gain +1 extra Food from each Fertile space containing a Settler (and no city) that you collect from, up to 2 extra Food per Collect action.
• Sprawling (Husbandry). After building one or more Settlers or using Sanitation, move any or all of your Settlers currently in cities at no action cost.
• Fireworks (Chemistry). Pay 1 Ore before the first combat round to prevent your opponent from playing a Combat card that round.
• Fighting Bands (Any Government). Once per turn as a free action, exchange any of your Settlers into Infantry and any of your Infantry into Settlers.

Egypt

• Flood Plains (Irrigation). Collect Food or Wood from Barren spaces and found Settlements on them.
• Architecture (Engineering). Replace resources or Culture tokens 1:1 with Mood tokens when building Wonders or increasing city size.
• Embalming (Rituals). Gain 1 Culture token after fighting any battle if you have 4 or fewer Culture tokens, and always gain 1 Culture token when your Leader dies.
• Man God (Priesthood). Each Theocracy advance also grants the power of the Autocracy advance directly to its left.

Greece

• Formal Training (Public Education). Gain 1 Idea whenever you build units in a city with an Academy.
• Spartans (Draft). Spend Culture tokens instead of Mood tokens for Draft, and prevent attackers from playing Action cards in combat rounds where you have fewer Army units.
• Hellenization (Art & Sculptures). Attempt Cultural Influence from foreign cities under your influence, and activate Art & Sculptures using 2 Mood tokens.
• City States (Voting). Once per turn, avoid a mood drop when activating a city a second time by letting another city of at least equal size and mood count as activated instead.

Huns

• Nomads (Storage). Activate happy size 1 cities to move into enemy-free Land spaces and gain 1 matching resource from the new space.
• Mounted Archers (Husbandry). All cities can build Cavalry, and your Cavalry do not have to be eliminated before Infantry.
• Raiders (Tactics). Opponent Settlers within 2 spaces of a Hun Army cannot found cities or form trade routes unless accompanied by an Army, Leader, or city.
• Hunnic Tribes (Any Government). Pay 1 Culture token or 1 Gold to cancel a Barbarian attack against you or redirect it toward an eligible opponent city.

India

• Indian Elephants (Husbandry). All cities can build Elephants, and Elephants may establish trade routes unless they are in an angry city.
• Proselytism (State Religion). Settlers can make Cultural Influence attempts at range 2, and each Settler adds +1 range to a city or fellow Settler in its space.
• Prosperity (Trade Routes). Trade routes may generate Mood tokens instead of Food or Gold, or Culture tokens from target cities under your Cultural Influence.
• Peace and Poetry (Drama & Music). Once per turn, pay 1 Mood token instead of 2 Food when purchasing an advance.

Japan

• Pottery (Storage). Once per turn, gain 1 Culture token when a city collects at least 3 Food.
• Horsemanship (Husbandry). All cities can build Cavalry, and Armies with Cavalry may reroll 1 die in the first combat round by paying 1 Culture token (free if you have Rituals).
• Subterfuge (Tactics). Discard an Action card to cancel any non-combat Action card played by an opponent within 2 spaces of your cities or Army units.
• Shogunate (Any Government). Once per turn, play a free-action Action card at no action cost, and use Draft to draw an Action card in place of a unit.

Maya

• Terracing (Irrigation). Collect Ore, Wood, or Food from Mountain spaces, and allow Settlers and Leaders to ignore Mountain terrain penalties.
• Stelas (Art & Sculptures). Gain 1 Culture token or 1 Idea whenever you increase city size with an Obelisk, found a city, or capture a city.
• Ballcourts (Circus & Sports). Pay 1 Mood token to treat a city as +1 size when collecting or building units, and reduce the cost of Spirituality advances by 1 Food once per turn.
• Calendar (Astronomy). Look at the top Event card at any time, and pay 1 Culture token to place it at the bottom of the deck.

Persia

• Elephants (Husbandry). All cities can build Elephants, and each Elephant cancels the bonus of one enemy Cavalry in the first round of combat.
• Immortals (Draft). Spend up to 4 Culture tokens prior to each combat round for +1 Combat Value per token.
• Zoroastrianism (Priesthood). Target an enemy Army unit in a Land space without cities using Cultural Influence to convert it to your color and battle remaining enemies there.
• Banking (Currency). At the start of your turn before trade routes, gain 1 Gold or 1 Culture token if you already have any Gold.

Phoenicia

• City Independence (Fishing). All Ports in your color gain the Obelisk power and score 1½ VP each, while your Government advances are worth 0 VP.
• Biremes (Warships). Deal +1 hit in the first round of naval combat against opponents (including Pirates) who lack Warships.
• Alphabet (Writing). Up to 2 trade routes may each generate either 1 Culture token or 2 Ideas instead of Food or Gold.
• Cedars and Dyes (Trade Routes). Your Ships and cities with Ports can establish trade routes with coastal cities up to 3 spaces away.

Rome

• Aqueducts (Engineering). Ignore Famine events, and buy Sanitation at either no Food cost or no action cost.
• Imperial Roads (Roads). Once per turn as a free action, move one unit or group between two Roman cities using Roads at no resource cost.
• Slave Economy (Bartering). Spend Mood tokens as any resource when increasing city size, and gain 1 Gold and 1 Mood token after winning any battle.
• Provinces (Any Government). All cities can build Cavalry, and upon capturing a city you may immediately make it neutral—or happy by paying 1 Culture token.

Vikings

• Shipcraft (Fishing). Infantry and Settlers may move into an adjacent empty Sea space to convert into Ships (and vice versa), and Navigation costs no Food.
• Longships (Warships). Ships may move even if they battled this turn, and Ships can carry up to 3 Land units.
• Runestones (Rituals). After any battle where you lose 2 or more Army units, place an unused Obelisk in front of you as a 1 VP objective.
• Marauders (Trade Routes). When your Ships pair with adjacent opponent cities for Trade Routes, each targeted city owner loses 1 resource of their choice.
